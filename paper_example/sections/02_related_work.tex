\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Financial multi-agent systems.}
LLM-based multi-agent frameworks for financial decision-making have advanced rapidly. TradingAgents~\cite{tradingagents2025} coordinates fundamental, sentiment, and technical analysts through bull-bear debate. FinCon~\cite{fincon2024} introduces a manager-analyst hierarchy with conceptual verbal reinforcement and dual-level risk control. FinAgent~\cite{finagent2024} incorporates chart images as multimodal inputs without structured geometric extraction. R\&D-Agent-Quant~\cite{rdagent2025} organizes factor research into a Research-Development-Feedback loop with multi-armed bandit scheduling. AlphaAgent~\cite{alphaagent2025} mines alpha expressions through AST-based originality constraints. FinTeam~\cite{finteam2025} and MAS4TS~\cite{mas4ts2025} extend multi-agent collaboration to comprehensive financial scenarios and general time series tasks, respectively. All these systems improve exclusively at the prompt level; none propagates trajectory-level rewards to model parameters.
% 中文：基于 LLM 的金融多智能体框架发展迅速。TradingAgents 通过多空辩论协调基本面、情绪和技术分析师。FinCon 引入管理者-分析师层级与概念性语言强化和双层风控。FinAgent 将图表图像作为多模态输入但未做结构化几何提取。RD-Agent-Quant 将因子研究组织为研究-开发-反馈循环并用多臂赌博机调度。AlphaAgent 通过 AST 原创性约束挖掘 alpha 表达式。FinTeam 和 MAS4TS 分别将多智能体协作扩展到综合金融场景和通用时序任务。所有这些系统仅在 prompt 层面改进；没有一个将轨迹级奖励传播到模型参数。

\paragraph{Self-evolving LLM agents.}
SE-Agent~\cite{seagent2025} applies revision, recombination, and refinement on agent trajectories. SEEA-R1~\cite{seea2025} combines Monte Carlo tree search with GRPO. Self-Challenging Agents~\cite{selfchallenge2025} and Agent0~\cite{agent02026} use self-play for autonomous data generation. R-Zero~\cite{rzero2026} demonstrates cost-efficient trajectory recycling. These methods consistently outperform prompt-only alternatives but have not been adapted to finance, where sparse high-variance rewards, multi-agent credit assignment, and regime-dependent signals pose additional challenges.
% 中文：SE-Agent 对 agent 轨迹应用修订、重组和精炼操作。SEEA-R1 将蒙特卡洛树搜索与 GRPO 结合。Self-Challenging Agents 和 Agent0 使用自博弈进行自主数据生成。R-Zero 展示了高效的轨迹回收。这些方法一致优于仅 prompt 的替代方案，但尚未适配到金融领域——那里稀疏高方差奖励、多智能体信用分配和 regime 相关信号带来额外挑战。

\paragraph{On-policy distillation.}
Generalized Knowledge Distillation~\cite{gkd2024} establishes that on-policy student samples yield tighter bounds than off-policy teacher samples. Self-Distilled Reasoner~\cite{opsd2026} shows that a single model can serve as both teacher and student through differential conditioning on privileged information, using token-level JSD with per-token KL clipping. CRISP~\cite{crisp2026} extends this to iterative rounds. DPO~\cite{dpo2023} and GRPO~\cite{grpo2024} provide preference optimization foundations. We transfer on-policy self-distillation to financial multi-agent trajectories, redefining privileged information as hindsight risk-adjusted PnL to avoid the reward-hacking failure documented in~\cite{losingwinner2025}.
% 中文：广义知识蒸馏证明在线策略学生样本比离线教师样本产生更紧的近似界。Self-Distilled Reasoner 表明单一模型可通过对特权信息的差异条件化同时充当教师和学生，使用 token 级 JSD 和逐 token KL 裁剪。CRISP 将其扩展到迭代轮次。DPO 和 GRPO 提供偏好优化基础。我们将在线策略自蒸馏迁移到金融多智能体轨迹，将特权信息重新定义为事后风险调整 PnL 以避免文献中记录的 reward hacking 失败。

\paragraph{Financial chart understanding and evaluation rigor.}
FinChart-Bench~\cite{finchartbench2025} and ChartMuseum~\cite{chartmuseum2025} quantify a 30+ percentage point gap between VLMs and human experts on financial chart reasoning. Time-VLM~\cite{timevlm2025} and VisionTS~\cite{visionts2024} explore visual representations for time series forecasting. On the evaluation side, DeepFund~\cite{deepfund2025} demonstrates that strong backtest performance does not transfer to live trading due to pretraining leakage. Look-Ahead-Bench~\cite{lookaheadbench2026} and FactFin~\cite{factfin2025} propose temporal separation and counterfactual perturbation protocols. We adopt these as engineering commitments in our evaluation.
% 中文：FinChart-Bench 和 ChartMuseum 量化了 VLM 与人类专家在金融图表推理上 30+ 个百分点的差距。Time-VLM 和 VisionTS 探索视觉表示用于时序预测。在评估方面，DeepFund 证明强回测表现因预训练数据泄漏而无法迁移到实盘交易。Look-Ahead-Bench 和 FactFin 提出时间分离和反事实扰动协议。我们将这些作为评估中的工程承诺。
