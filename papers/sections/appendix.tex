\subsection{Chart Geometry Extraction Algorithms}
\label{app:geometry}

\paragraph{Trendline detection.}
Local extrema are identified as bars whose value is the maximum (or minimum) within a window of $\pm 5$ bars. Consecutive plateau values (equal-valued extrema within the window) are deduplicated by retaining only the first occurrence. A least-squares line is fitted to the extrema positions, and lines with $R^2 \leq 0.3$ are discarded. The system fits trendlines over multiple sub-windows (full lookback, half-lookback) to detect both long-range and short-range trends. A trendline is tagged as ``broken'' if the current price exceeds the extrapolated line value by more than 1\%.

\paragraph{Support and resistance.}
Pivot prices are extracted from local highs and lows (order 3). These pivots are clustered by proximity, with the tolerance set to 2\% of the full price range to adapt across different asset price scales. Clusters with fewer than 2 touches are discarded. Each resulting level is classified as support (below current price) or resistance (above current price), with levels that have been recently broken reclassified as ``broken support'' or ``broken resistance''.

\paragraph{Candlestick patterns.}
The system classifies individual bars and two-bar combinations within the most recent configurable window (default 10 bars). Classification uses body-to-range ratios and shadow proportions: doji (body ratio $< 0.1$), hammer (lower shadow $> 2 \times$ body, bullish close), shooting star (upper shadow $> 2 \times$ body, bearish close), and engulfing patterns (current body envelops previous bar with opposite direction).

\paragraph{Market regime classification.}
The regime classifier uses two features computed from the most recent 20 bars: annualised volatility (standard deviation of daily returns $\times \sqrt{252}$) and trend strength (the relative gap between SMA(5) and SMA(20)). The volatility is normalised against the full-sample standard deviation to obtain a percentile. Regimes are assigned as: volatile (volatility percentile $> 0.7$), trending up (trend strength $> 0.02$), trending down (trend strength $< -0.02$), ranging ($|$trend strength$|$ $< 0.01$), or quiet (otherwise).

\subsection{Agent Prompt Templates}
\label{app:prompts}

Below we provide representative prompt excerpts for the ChartAnalyst and DecisionPM agents. Full prompt templates are available in the code repository.

\paragraph{ChartAnalyst system prompt (excerpt).}
\begin{quote}
\small
You are a professional chart analyst specialising in technical analysis of financial candlestick charts. Analyse the provided chart image and identify: (1) overall trend direction and strength, (2) key support and resistance levels, (3) notable chart formations (double top/bottom, triangles, head-and-shoulders), (4) candlestick patterns at the current price, (5) volume patterns and any divergences. Provide your assessment as structured JSON.
\end{quote}

\paragraph{DecisionPM system prompt (excerpt).}
\begin{quote}
\small
You are a portfolio manager synthesising inputs from multiple specialist analysts. Given chart analysis, pattern assessment, event context, and risk evaluation, produce a final trading decision (buy/sell/hold) with conviction level, position size recommendation, and a concise rationale that addresses any disagreements between analysts.
\end{quote}
