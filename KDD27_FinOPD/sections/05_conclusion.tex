\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textsc{FinOPD}, a financial multi-agent framework that enables policy-level self-evolution through on-policy self-distillation conditioned on hindsight risk-adjusted PnL, Shapley-weighted credit assignment across specialist agents, and a non-parametric belief store that complements parametric adaptation. Experiments on Dow-30 and CSI-300 benchmarks demonstrate state-of-the-art Sharpe ratio, drawdown control, and cross-regime robustness, with sustained improvement over self-evolution iterations under strict post-cutoff evaluation.
