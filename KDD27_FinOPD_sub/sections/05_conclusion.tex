\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\textsc{FinOPD} closes the financial-agent learning loop by distilling post-horizon estimated hindsight from current-policy trajectories while keeping deployment strictly point-in-time. The framework coordinates a frozen, auditable alpha-factor library, agent-weighted OPD, time-safe episodic memory, and regime-aware selective execution. Under a shared backbone and identical retrospective point-in-time protocol, \textsc{FinOPD} ranks first among 13 methods in macro-averaged per-asset return, Sharpe, and Calmar. These summaries are descriptive rather than portfolio-level or live-trading evidence; the accompanying evaluator makes synchronized PnL and dependence-aware uncertainty mandatory for future portfolio claims.
