\section*{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

The deterministic evaluation does not establish statistical reliability across independent market cycles or causally separate OPD from episodic memory. The hindsight utility may also be misspecified despite a valid temporal boundary. \textsc{FinOPD} is a research prototype, not investment advice. Deployment would require licensed point-in-time data, human approval, exposure limits, and market-impact and operational-risk tests.
