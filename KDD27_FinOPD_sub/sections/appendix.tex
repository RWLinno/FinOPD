\subsection{Agent Roles and Prompt Contract}

The four evidence specialists and DecisionPM exchange typed messages through decision-local Gated Shared Memory. DecisionPM writes the fifth proposal; the constrained allocation head then applies the selective-execution controls that produce $\mathbf w_t$. This coordination state is reset after each decision and is distinct from Time-Safe Episodic Memory, which admits only matured episodes after horizon closure.
% 中文翻译：四个证据专门化智能体与 DecisionPM 通过决策局部的 Gated Shared Memory 交换类型化消息。DecisionPM 写入第五个决策建议，受约束配置头随后应用选择性执行控制并生成 $\mathbf w_t$。该协调状态在每次决策后重置，与 Time-Safe Episodic Memory 不同；后者只在结果窗口闭合后接纳已经成熟的情节。

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{ChartAnalyst} converts structured chart geometry into directional evidence with provenance and confidence.
    % 中文翻译（条目）：ChartAnalyst 把结构化图形几何转化为带有来源和置信度的方向证据。
    \item \textbf{PatternReasoner} reconciles patterns across daily, weekly, and monthly horizons and exposes conflicts.
    % 中文翻译（条目）：PatternReasoner 协调日、周、月多个周期的形态，并显式暴露冲突。
    \item \textbf{EventAnalyst} contextualizes point-in-time news, earnings, and filing signals.
    % 中文翻译（条目）：EventAnalyst 对时点可得的新闻、财报和公告信号进行语境化分析。
    \item \textbf{RiskController} checks drawdown, volatility, and concentration constraints and may veto exposure.
    % 中文翻译（条目）：RiskController 检查回撤、波动率与集中度约束，并可以否决风险暴露。
    \item \textbf{DecisionPM} fuses the typed evidence into action, position size, and risk controls.
    % 中文翻译（条目）：DecisionPM 把类型化证据融合为动作、仓位大小和风险控制。
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Teacher-only extension.} After horizon $H$ closes, additionally read an estimated hindsight summary of cost-adjusted return, drawdown, CVaR, and turnover. Do not receive or reconstruct raw future OHLCV arrays, price paths, or future charts.
% 中文翻译：教师仅在结果窗口 $H$ 闭合后额外读取成本调整收益、回撤、CVaR 与换手率的估计事后摘要，不得接收或重建未来原始 OHLCV 数组、价格路径或图表。

\textbf{Information boundary.} The teacher-only field is absent from every student and deployment payload. The rationale must cite supplied evidence rather than unseen market data.
% 中文翻译：所有学生与部署负载都不包含教师专用字段；决策理由必须引用已提供证据，而不能引用未见市场数据。

\subsection{Frozen Factor Library}

Alpha-Factor Evolution is completed before OPD. The resulting manifest contains 157 admitted executable records; after transaction costs, mean $|\mathrm{IR}|$ is 1.1918, median is 1.1097, and the maximum is 3.0401. Among them, 87 satisfy $|\mathrm{IR}|\geq1$. The router may change selection weights during OPD, but the manifest hash, expressions, and record count remain fixed. This separation prevents the policy-training stage from being mistaken for continued factor mining.
% 中文翻译：Alpha-Factor Evolution 在 OPD 之前完成。最终清单包含 157 条已准入的可执行记录；计入交易成本后，平均 $|\mathrm{IR}|$ 为 1.1918，中位数为 1.1097，最大值为 3.0401，其中 87 条满足 $|\mathrm{IR}|\geq1$。OPD 期间路由器可以改变选择权重，但清单哈希、表达式内容和记录数量保持不变。这一分离避免把策略训练阶段误解为继续进行因子挖掘。

\subsection{Illustrative Decision Trace}
\label{sec:case_study}

We trace one anonymized asset-day from the held-out test window. ChartAnalyst reported bearish geometry with confidence 0.80; PatternReasoner was neutral at 0.42; EventAnalyst supplied no strong event signal (neutral, 0.10); and RiskController judged risk moderate and allowed the proposal to proceed.
% 中文翻译：我们追踪留出测试窗口中的一个匿名资产日。ChartAnalyst 以 0.80 的置信度报告看跌几何形态；PatternReasoner 以 0.42 的置信度保持中性；EventAnalyst 没有提供强事件信号（中性，0.10）；RiskController 判断风险适中并允许方案继续。

DecisionPM combined these messages into a score of $-0.384$ and a risk-adjusted score of $-0.307$, crossing the sell-side threshold and assigning 12.6\% exposure. Gated Shared Memory marked a chart--factor disagreement, so the manager preserved the higher-confidence chart evidence while recording the conflict. The example illustrates typed arbitration and risk-scaled sizing; it is explanatory rather than a claim of representativeness.
% 中文翻译：DecisionPM 将这些消息融合为 $-0.384$ 的原始得分和 $-0.307$ 的风险调整得分，越过卖出阈值并配置 12.6\% 的风险暴露。Gated Shared Memory 标记了图形--因子分歧，因此管理器保留置信度更高的图形证据，同时记录冲突。该案例用于解释类型化仲裁与按风险缩放的仓位，并不声称具有统计代表性。

\section{Expanded Limitations and Ethical Discussion}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scope and uncertainty.}
The evaluation covers four liquid U.S. equities, daily decisions, one 17-month test period, and one selected training run. Deterministic decoding removes test-time sampling noise, but it does not quantify optimization variance or provide statistical confidence intervals across independent market histories. The regime results contain only the regimes realized in this period, and the joint evolution trace does not isolate the causal contribution of OPD and episodic memory. Broader universes, repeated training runs, multiple non-overlapping market cycles, and a factorial evolution ablation are needed before claiming general market robustness.
% 中文翻译：评估覆盖四只高流动性美国股票、日频决策、一个 17 个月测试期和一次选定训练运行。确定性解码能够消除测试时采样噪声，但不能量化优化过程方差，也不能提供跨独立市场历史的统计置信区间。状态结果只包含这一时期实际出现的市场状态，联合进化轨迹也没有隔离 OPD 与情节记忆各自的因果贡献。在宣称一般性市场稳健性之前，还需要更广泛的股票池、重复训练运行、多个互不重叠的市场周期以及针对进化轨道的析因消融。

\paragraph{Information boundaries and reproducibility.}
The teacher depends on a designed hindsight summary and utility, so misspecification can transfer undesirable preferences even when the temporal boundary is valid. The reference KL guard limits drift but does not guarantee safety under structural breaks. Reproduction also requires point-in-time market inputs, transaction-cost conventions, and versioned factor manifests; changing any of them changes the estimand.
% 中文翻译：教师依赖人工设计的事后摘要与效用，因此即使时间边界有效，设定错误仍可能传递不理想的偏好。参考策略 KL 约束能够限制漂移，但无法保证在结构突变下的安全性。复现还需要时点一致的市场输入、交易成本约定与版本化因子清单；改变其中任何一项都会改变被估计对象。
