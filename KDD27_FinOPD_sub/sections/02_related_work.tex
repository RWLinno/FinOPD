\paragraph{Financial trading agents.}
Financial LLM research has progressed from domain pretraining and instruction tuning~\cite{wu2023bloomberggpt,liu2023fingpt,li2023large} toward agents that organize heterogeneous market evidence. Role-specialized agent systems assign technical, fundamental, sentiment, trading, and risk responsibilities to interacting agents, using debate or managerial hierarchies to reconcile recommendations~\cite{tradingagents2025,fincon2024,hedgeagents2025,finteam2025}. Alpha-discovery systems automate the quantitative research loop: RD-Agent(Q) jointly explores factors and models, AlphaAgent regularizes expressions against redundancy and alpha decay, and newer systems accumulate reusable skills or constrain generation through executable operator languages~\cite{rdagent2025,alphaagent2025,factorminer2026,hubble2026}. Multimodal tool-augmented systems connect language reasoning to numerical feeds, financial tools, news, and charts~\cite{finagent2024,finrobot2024}; FinChart-Bench shows that financial chart comprehension itself remains challenging for current vision-language models~\cite{finchartbench2025}. These paradigms expand coordination, research automation, and evidence coverage, but do not directly distill the action policy from dense feedback on its own matured portfolio trajectories. \textsc{FinOPD} freezes factor research before training and updates the decision policy only from subsequent current-policy trajectories whose outcomes have matured.
% 中文翻译：金融 LLM 研究已经从领域预训练与指令微调发展到能够组织异构市场证据的智能体。角色专门化智能体系统把技术面、基本面、情绪、交易和风险职责分配给多个交互智能体，并通过辩论或管理层级协调建议。Alpha 发现系统自动化量化研究循环：RD-Agent(Q) 联合探索因子与模型，AlphaAgent 通过冗余与 Alpha 衰减约束来规范表达式，更新的系统还会积累可复用技能，或通过可执行算子语言约束生成。多模态工具增强系统把语言推理连接到数值数据流、金融工具、新闻和图表；FinChart-Bench 则表明，金融图表理解本身对当前视觉语言模型仍具有挑战。这些范式扩展了协同、研究自动化与证据覆盖能力，但没有根据自身成熟组合轨迹上的稠密反馈直接蒸馏动作策略。FinOPD 在训练前冻结因子研究，并且只利用结果已经成熟的后续当前策略轨迹更新决策策略。

\paragraph{Self-evolving agents.}
Agent self-improvement spans two broad mechanisms. Non-parametric approaches retain trial feedback outside model weights: Reflexion stores linguistic critiques, generative agents combine experience streams with reflection and retrieval, and cognitive architectures separate working, episodic, semantic, and procedural memory~\cite{reflexion2023,park2023generative,sumers2023cognitive}. Financial systems instantiate this principle through layered market memory, evolving knowledge bases, or reusable factor-mining experience~\cite{yu2023finmem,quantagent2024,factorminer2026}. Parametric approaches modify the learner through verbal feedback, policy-level reflection, trajectory optimization, or bootstrapped training experience~\cite{stephan2024rlvf,zhang2024agent,seagent2025,sirius2025,rzero2026}; SHARP instead constrains financial self-improvement to atomic edits of an auditable symbolic rubric~\cite{sharp2026}. External memory preserves rare, inspectable cases, whereas parameter learning internalizes transferable behavior; neither alone determines when delayed portfolio outcomes become valid evidence. \textsc{FinOPD} updates the policy from matured trajectories while admitting only availability-filtered, high-utility episodes to non-parametric memory.
% 中文翻译：智能体自我改进大体包含两类机制。非参数方法把试错反馈保存在模型权重之外：Reflexion 保存语言批评，generative agents 把经验流与反思和检索结合，认知架构则区分工作记忆、情节记忆、语义记忆和程序记忆。金融系统通过分层市场记忆、持续演化的知识库或可复用的因子挖掘经验来实现这一原则。参数方法则通过语言反馈、策略级反思、轨迹优化或自举训练经验修改学习器；SHARP 采用另一条路径，把金融自我改进限制为对可审计符号规则的原子化编辑。外部记忆有利于保留稀有且可检查的案例，参数学习则能内化可迁移行为；但两者本身都没有规定延迟组合结果何时成为有效证据。FinOPD 利用已经成熟的轨迹更新策略，同时只把通过可用时间过滤且效用较高的情节写入非参数记忆。

\paragraph{On-policy distillation.}
Classical knowledge distillation transfers a teacher distribution on a fixed corpus~\cite{hinton2015distilling}, and policy distillation extends the principle to action policies~\cite{rusu2015policy}. Offline teacher sequences, however, need not cover prefixes induced by the deployed student. GKD queries the teacher on student-generated samples and provides the closest general on-policy foundation~\cite{gkd2024}. Recent variants condition teachers on privileged reasoning, combine dense transfer with verifiable rewards, construct proximal teachers, or gate teacher logits with verifier feedback~\cite{selfdistilledreasoner2026,rlsd2026,topd2026,rgopd2026}. SEED further converts completed agent trajectories into hindsight skills before distilling their token-level effect~\cite{seed2026}, while DPO and GRPO provide complementary sequence-level preference objectives~\cite{dpo2023,grpo2024}. These methods target reasoning or agent tasks with response-level verification; financial decisions expose delayed portfolio utility shared across interacting agents. \textsc{FinOPD} uses a separate frozen teacher restricted to post-horizon estimated hindsight, agent-weighted token alignment, and a frozen-reference KL guard.
Two concurrent 2026 preprints sharpen this boundary. MAD-OPD uses multi-teacher debate and task-adaptive divergence to improve agentic token supervision~\cite{wang2026madopd}; our 27B teacher remains single and frozen, while coalition replay attributes one financial trajectory's utility across structured contributors. SERL studies which environment feedback to insert and where in a multi-turn trajectory~\cite{li2026selective}; our gate is instead imposed by market-time availability and portfolio-outcome closure, and the teacher receives only five aggregate outcome scalars. These are close methodological neighbors, so our novelty claim is limited to the finance-specific temporal, routing, memory, and credit integration rather than OPD itself.
% 中文翻译：经典知识蒸馏在固定语料上迁移教师分布，策略蒸馏则把这一原则扩展到动作策略。然而，离线教师序列未必覆盖部署学生自身诱导出的前缀。GKD 在学生生成的样本上查询教师，是与本文最接近的通用 on-policy 基础。近期方法会让教师接收特权推理信息、把稠密迁移与可验证奖励结合、构造近端教师，或用验证器反馈门控教师 logits。SEED 还会先把已经完成的智能体轨迹转换为事后技能，再蒸馏其 token 级影响；DPO 与 GRPO 则提供互补的序列级偏好目标。这些方法主要面向能够进行响应级验证的推理或智能体任务；金融决策得到的却是由多个交互智能体共享、延迟到达的组合效用。FinOPD 使用仅限于结果窗口闭合后估计事后信息的独立冻结教师，并加入智能体加权 token 对齐和冻结参考策略 KL 约束。
