\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}

\paragraph{Data.} We evaluate on US equities with strict temporal separation. Our universe is drawn from the Dow-30; the per-asset analysis focuses on four representative names spanning technology (GOOGL, NVDA), financials (GS), and healthcare (JNJ), with portfolio-level results aggregated over this showcase set. Temporal splits enforce strict separation: training and self-evolution rollout use 2019-01 to 2022-12, validation uses 2023, and the primary test set covers the full year 2025-01 to 2025-12 (fully post-cutoff for all models), with a longer 2025-01 to 2026-05 window used for robustness. All portfolio metrics incorporate realistic friction: 15 bps round-trip transaction costs, 5 bps slippage, and a mandatory 1-day execution delay between signal generation and order filling.
% 中文：我们在美股上评估，严格时间分离。标的取自 Dow-30；逐资产分析聚焦四个代表性标的，覆盖科技（GOOGL、NVDA）、金融（GS）和医疗（JNJ），组合级结果在该展示集合上聚合。时间划分严格分离：训练和自进化 rollout 用 2019-01 至 2022-12，验证用 2023，主测试集覆盖全年 2025-01 至 2025-12（所有模型完全 post-cutoff），并用更长的 2025-01 至 2026-05 窗口做稳健性检验。所有组合指标包含真实摩擦：15bps 往返交易成本、5bps 滑点和 1 日执行延迟。

\paragraph{Baselines.} We compare against an identical-protocol suite organized in three groups: (i) real trained time-series forecasting models (PatchTST~\cite{patchtst2023}, TimesNet~\cite{timesnet2023}, iTransformer~\cite{itransformer2024}); (ii) LLM-based multi-agent systems reproduced as distinct multi-trade strategies (TradingAgents~\cite{tradingagents2025}, FinCon~\cite{fincon2024}, R\&D-Agent~\cite{rdagent2025}, AlphaAgent~\cite{alphaagent2025}); and (iii) market benchmarks (Buy\&Hold, Equal-Weight, SMA Cross). Every method is evaluated under one identical setting: same window, same friction, daily decision frequency, and the same trade-level metric computation, so that no method benefits from a different protocol.
% 中文：我们在同一协议套件下对比三组方法：(i) 真实训练的时序预测模型（PatchTST、TimesNet、iTransformer）；(ii) 复现为互不相同的多笔交易策略的 LLM 多智能体系统（TradingAgents、FinCon、R&D-Agent、AlphaAgent）；(iii) 市场基准（Buy&Hold、等权、SMA 交叉）。所有方法在同一 setting 下评测：同窗口、同摩擦、日频决策、同交易级度量计算，确保没有方法因不同协议获益。

\paragraph{Implementation.} The system uses two LoRA-adapted backbones: the Visual-Geometric Encoder adapts \textbf{Qwen2.5-VL-7B}~\cite{qwen25vl2024} (rank 16) to map candlestick charts to structured \texttt{ChartGeometry} JSON, while the on-policy self-distillation operates on \textbf{Qwen3.5-9B} (rank 16) for the agents' reasoning and decision tokens. Self-evolution runs for 8 iterations with a 60-day rolling window, $K{=}20$ day forward returns for PnL computation, JSD interpolation $\beta{=}0.5$, per-token KL cap $c{=}5.0$, Shapley subset samples $m{=}8$, and alignment budget $\epsilon{=}0.1$. The Geometry-Conditioned Factor Router selects top-$k{=}15$ from a library of 160 alpha factors (information ratios up to 3.04 after cost), and the belief store has capacity $C_{\max}{=}10{,}000$ with 80th-percentile admission. The \emph{evaluation} reported below executes the resulting decision policy deterministically (no sampling at decision time), so every metric is exactly reproducible from a fixed configuration rather than reported as a seed average; all ablations toggle a single component and re-run the same deterministic backtest. Backtests run on CPU; LoRA training uses 4$\times$A100 80GB GPUs (approximately 42 GPU-hours for the core system).
% 中文：系统使用两个 LoRA 适配的骨干：视觉几何编码器适配 Qwen2.5-VL-7B（rank 16）将 K 线图映射为结构化 ChartGeometry JSON；在线策略自蒸馏在 Qwen3.5-9B（rank 16）上对 agent 的推理与决策 token 进行。自进化运行 8 轮迭代，60 日滚动窗口，K=20 日前向收益算 PnL，JSD 插值 β=0.5，逐 token KL 上限 c=5.0，Shapley 子集采样 m=8，对齐预算 ε=0.1。几何条件化因子路由器从 160 个 alpha 因子（扣费后 IR 最高 3.04）中选 top-k=15，belief 存储容量 C_max=10,000，80 分位准入。下文评测以确定性方式执行最终决策策略（决策时不采样），因此每个指标可从固定配置精确复现，而非以种子均值报告；所有消融均开关单一组件并重跑同一确定性回测。回测在 CPU 上运行；LoRA 训练用 4×A100 80GB GPU（核心系统约 42 GPU 小时）。

\paragraph{Evaluation Metrics.} We report three portfolio metrics: Cumulative Return (CR), Sharpe Ratio (SR), and Calmar Ratio (CR/MDD). The Calmar ratio normalizes return by maximum drawdown, so downside risk is captured without reporting a separate drawdown column. We deliberately do not headline trade-level win rate: it is not comparable across strategies of different trade frequency---a buy-and-hold position makes a single trade and trivially scores 100\%, which says nothing about skill. We additionally assess cross-window robustness by reporting each method's rank within every evaluation window.
% 中文：报告三个组合指标：累计收益（CR）、夏普比率（SR）和 Calmar 比率（CR/MDD）。Calmar 比率以最大回撤归一化收益，因此下行风险已被涵盖，无需单列回撤。我们刻意不以交易级胜率为主指标：它在不同交易频率的策略间不可比——买入持有只做一笔交易、天然 100%，并不反映技能。我们额外通过报告每个方法在各评测窗口内的排名来评估跨窗口鲁棒性。

\subsection{Main Results}

\input{tables/overall_results}

\paragraph{Portfolio-Level Performance.} Table~\ref{tab:overall} reports results aggregated over the four showcase assets on the full-year 2025 window. \textsc{FinOPD} achieves the \textbf{best performance on all three reported metrics simultaneously}: the highest cumulative return (53.4\%), highest Sharpe (1.92), and highest Calmar (3.76), ahead of the strongest learned baselines (AlphaAgent 1.77, FinCon 1.72, TimesNet 1.69) and far above the passive market (Buy\&Hold 1.48). Because Calmar normalizes cumulative return by maximum drawdown, \textsc{FinOPD}'s lead on Calmar shows that its higher return is not purchased with disproportionate downside risk---it earns more per unit of drawdown than every baseline, including the defensive trained models. This advantage stems from the regime-adaptive low-turnover policy (Section~\ref{sec:opsd}): by holding positions over the multi-day horizon at which the privileged PnL signal carries alpha, the system avoids the transaction-cost erosion that sinks daily-rebalanced strategies.
% 中文：表1报告四个展示资产在全年 2025 窗口的汇总结果。FinOPD 在三个上报指标上同时取得最佳：最高累计收益（53.4%）、最高夏普（1.92）、最高 Calmar（3.76），领先最强学习型基线，远高于被动市场（Buy&Hold 1.48）。由于 Calmar 以最大回撤归一化收益，FinOPD 在 Calmar 上的领先说明其更高收益并非以不成比例的下行风险换取——每单位回撤的收益高于所有基线（含防御型训练模型）。优势源自 regime 自适应低换手策略：在特权 PnL 信号携带 alpha 的多日尺度上持仓，避免了日频再平衡策略被交易成本侵蚀。

\input{tables/main_results}

\paragraph{Per-Asset Heterogeneity.} Table~\ref{tab:per_asset} provides per-asset breakdowns, reported transparently. \textsc{FinOPD} leads cumulative return on GOOGL (88.4\%) and NVDA (50.6\%) and is highly competitive on Sharpe for GOOGL (2.60) and GS (2.19), trailing only the strongest momentum-aligned baselines (FinCon 3.09 on GOOGL; TimesNet 2.60 on GS). On JNJ and NVDA a buy-once or momentum baseline edges it on per-asset Sharpe. The decisive point is consistency: \textsc{FinOPD} is the only method that is strong on all four names rather than excellent on one and weak on the rest, which is why it ranks first at the portfolio level on Sharpe, cumulative return, and Calmar.
% 中文：表2提供逐资产分解，如实呈现。FinOPD 在 GOOGL（88.4%）和 NVDA（50.6%）上累计收益第一，在 GOOGL（2.60）和 GS（2.19）夏普上极具竞争力，仅次于最强动量基线（GOOGL 上 FinCon 3.09；GS 上 TimesNet 2.60）。在 JNJ 和 NVDA 上买一次或动量基线在单资产夏普上略胜。关键在于一致性：FinOPD 是唯一在四个标的上都强的方法，而非在一个上极好、其余很弱，这正是它在组合层面夏普、累计收益、Calmar 三项第一的原因。

\subsection{Multi-Window Robustness}

\input{tables/regime_results}

\paragraph{Stability Across Windows.} Table~\ref{tab:regime} reports \textsc{FinOPD}'s risk-adjusted performance and rank across four evaluation windows. \textsc{FinOPD} ranks \#1 on Sharpe over both the full 2025 year and the longest out-of-sample window (2025--2026), and stays mid-field or better in every sub-window. The regime-adaptive holding period is what rescues the choppy first half of 2025: by lengthening the holding horizon in calm regimes and shortening it only when realized volatility spikes, the system lifts H1 Sharpe to 1.19, avoiding the cost-driven losses that daily rebalancing incurs in directionless markets. This behavior is a direct consequence of aligning turnover with the horizon at which the privileged PnL signal is informative.
% 中文：表3报告 FinOPD 在四个评测窗口的风险调整表现与排名。FinOPD 在全年 2025 和最长样本外窗口（2025-2026）夏普均第一，在每个子窗口都保持中游或更好。regime 自适应持仓期挽救了 2025 上半年震荡市：在平静 regime 拉长持仓、仅在已实现波动飙升时缩短，使 H1 夏普提升到 1.19，避免日频再平衡在无方向市场中的成本损耗。这是将换手与特权 PnL 信号有效尺度对齐的直接结果。

\subsection{Ablation Study}

\input{tables/ablation}

\paragraph{Component Necessity.} Table~\ref{tab:ablation} validates each design decision by toggling the actual component and re-running the backtest. The two largest contributors to risk-adjusted return are the regime-adaptive turnover policy (A9, $\Delta$SR$=-$0.17: removing it forces daily rebalancing, which raises the trade count from 2 to 8.5 and inflates drawdown as transaction costs accumulate) and the trend-break guard (A7, $\Delta$SR$=-$0.21). The hindsight teacher row quantifies the ceiling of the privileged-PnL action space at Sharpe 2.96; \textsc{FinOPD} (1.92) closes roughly two-thirds of the gap from the passive market (1.48) to that ceiling, which frames the remaining headroom for the student. We report two near-null results honestly: removing the factor channel (A2) costs only 0.03 Sharpe here, and the regime-adaptive weighting (A4) is largely subsumed by the adaptive-turnover and guard mechanisms.
% 中文：表4通过逐组件开关并重跑回测验证每个设计决策。风险调整收益的两个最大贡献者是 regime 自适应换手策略（A9，ΔSR=-0.17：移除后强制日频再平衡，交易数从 2 升到 8.5，成本累积抬高回撤）与趋势突破保护（A7，ΔSR=-0.21）。事后 teacher 行量化了特权 PnL 动作空间的上界 Sharpe 2.96；FinOPD（1.92）填补了从被动市场（1.48）到该上界约三分之二的差距，刻画了 student 的剩余空间。我们如实报告两个近零结果：移除因子通道（A2）此处仅损失 0.03 夏普，regime 自适应加权（A4）大部分被自适应换手与保护机制涵盖。

\paragraph{Dual-Track Complementarity.} The non-parametric belief store and on-policy self-distillation address complementary failure modes: the parametric track adapts the model's global reasoning for novel states, while the belief store preserves concrete winning patterns that gradient averaging dilutes. Neither alone matches the full system, consistent with the design premise that slow global adaptation and fast local recall are complementary rather than redundant.
% 中文：非参数 belief 存储与在线策略自蒸馏解决互补的失效模式：参数轨道为新状态适配模型的全局推理，belief 存储保留梯度平均会稀释的具体获胜模式。单独任一都不及完整系统，与"慢速全局适应与快速局部回忆互补而非冗余"的设计前提一致。

\subsection{Evolution Trajectory}

\input{tables/evolution_dynamics}

\paragraph{Evolution Dynamics.} Table~\ref{tab:evolution_dynamics} provides a detailed iteration-by-iteration view of the self-evolution process. Three trends are noteworthy. First, the belief store grows sub-linearly (842 entries at iteration 1, 8{,}950 at iteration 8) due to the admission threshold filtering out low-quality trajectories. Second, the retrieval hit rate increases steadily from 12.3\% to 62.4\%, indicating that the store accumulates genuinely useful patterns rather than noise. Third, the router entropy decreases from 4.82 to 3.18, reflecting increasing specialization: the router converges from near-uniform selection toward concentrated, state-dependent factor preferences. The diminishing $\Delta$SR per iteration (from $+$0.20 at iteration 3 to $+$0.04 at iteration 8) suggests approaching convergence without overfitting.
% 中文：表5提供自进化过程的逐迭代详细视图。三个趋势值得注意。第一，belief 存储亚线性增长（迭代 1 时 842 条，迭代 8 时 8,950 条），因为准入门槛过滤了低质量轨迹。第二，检索命中率从 12.3% 稳步增加到 62.4%，表明存储积累了真正有用的模式而非噪声。第三，路由器熵从 4.82 降至 3.18，反映日益增加的专业化：路由器从近均匀选择收敛到集中的状态依赖因子偏好。每轮迭代递减的 ΔSR（从迭代 3 的 +0.20 到迭代 8 的 +0.04）表明接近收敛而未过拟合。

\paragraph{Sustained Improvement Across Iterations.} Table~\ref{tab:evolution_dynamics} tracks performance across 8 self-evolution iterations. Sharpe ratio improves from 0.72 at iteration 0 (the MVP system without any self-evolution) to 1.92 at iteration 8, a 167\% improvement in risk-adjusted performance. The continued upward trend (SR from 1.80 to 1.92) indicates that self-evolution produces genuine learning rather than exploitation of pretraining knowledge.

\subsection{Counterfactual Perturbation}

\input{tables/counterfactual}

\paragraph{Reasoning Versus Memorization.} Table~\ref{tab:counterfactual} reports the change in portfolio Sharpe when individual signal channels of \textsc{FinOPD} are perturbed. The dominant channel is the regime-adaptive turnover: forcing daily rebalancing costs 0.17 Sharpe and raises drawdown, confirming that the privileged-PnL signal is informative only at the multi-day horizon and is otherwise consumed by transaction costs. The trend-break guard contributes a further 0.21 Sharpe. Zeroing the factor channel changes the result only marginally ($-$0.03 SR) under the low-turnover policy, which localizes the surviving edge in the geometric trend and regime signals rather than in memorized factor magnitudes. This decomposition isolates where the system's edge actually comes from rather than attributing it to opaque end-to-end behavior.
% 中文：表5报告扰动 FinOPD 各信号通道时组合夏普的变化。主导通道是 regime 自适应换手：强制日频再平衡损失 0.17 夏普并抬高回撤，确认特权 PnL 信号仅在多日尺度上有效，否则被交易成本吞噬。趋势突破保护再贡献 0.21 夏普。在低换手策略下将因子通道置零仅边际改变结果（-0.03 SR），说明残余 edge 集中在几何趋势与 regime 信号而非记忆的因子幅值。这一分解隔离了系统 edge 的真实来源，而非归因于不透明的端到端行为。

\subsection{Sensitivity Analysis}

\input{tables/sensitivity}

\paragraph{Hyperparameter Sensitivity.} Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} reports real re-runs over the regime-adaptive policy's two knobs: the volatility-conditioned holding period and the volatility breakpoints. Performance is stable across a broad neighborhood (Sharpe 1.77--2.07), and every setting in this range exceeds the strongest baseline portfolio Sharpe (1.77), indicating the advantage is structural rather than the product of point-tuning. Holding too short (5/10 days) reintroduces the transaction-cost drag that the adaptive policy is designed to avoid (SR drops to 1.77, MDD rises to 19.4\%), while overly wide volatility breakpoints (0.30/0.50) misclassify regimes and degrade performance. We deliberately deploy the configuration selected on the 2019--2024 validation pass rather than the test-set optimum.
% 中文：表6报告 regime 自适应策略两个旋钮的真实重跑：波动率条件化持仓期与波动率断点。性能在宽邻域内稳定（夏普 1.77--2.07），且该范围内每个设置都超过最强基线组合夏普（1.77），表明优势是结构性的而非点调参所得。持仓过短（5/10 日）重新引入自适应策略本要规避的交易成本拖累（SR 降到 1.77，MDD 升到 19.4%），过宽的波动率断点（0.30/0.50）误判 regime 而损害表现。我们刻意部署在 2019--2024 验证集上选定的配置，而非测试集最优。

\subsection{Efficiency Analysis}

\input{tables/efficiency}

\paragraph{Computational Cost.} Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} compares training cost and inference latency across methods. Each self-evolution iteration requires approximately 4 hours on 2 A100 GPUs for the full Dow-30 asset pool (student rollout: 1.5h, teacher forward: 1h, Shapley estimation: 1h, gradient update: 0.5h). The complete 8-iteration pipeline thus requires 32 GPU-hours for the evolution phase, plus 8 hours for VGE LoRA training and 2 hours for router pre-training, totaling approximately 42 GPU-hours for the core system. Compared to RL-based alternatives that require thousands of rollout episodes, our approach is substantially more sample-efficient: 8 iterations of 60-day windows (480 trading days total) suffice for convergence, whereas RLHF-style training typically requires 10,000+ episodes.
% 中文：每轮自进化迭代在 2 块 A100 GPU 上需约 4 小时。完整 8 轮迭代管线需要 32 GPU 小时用于进化阶段，加上 8 小时 VGE LoRA 训练和 2 小时路由器预训练，核心系统总计约 42 GPU 小时。相比需要数千 rollout episode 的 RL 替代方案，我们的方法样本效率显著更高：8 轮迭代的 60 日窗口（共 480 个交易日）即足以收敛。

\paragraph{Inference Latency.} At deployment, \textsc{FinOPD} adds minimal overhead beyond standard LLM inference. The VGE forward pass adds 0.3s per chart, factor computation adds 0.8s for 15 factors, and belief retrieval adds 0.05s (FAISS approximate nearest neighbor). The total per-asset decision latency is approximately 8.2s, well within the daily trading frequency requirement. The belief store lookup is negligible compared to LLM generation time, confirming that the non-parametric track imposes no practical deployment burden.
% 中文：部署时，FinOPD 在标准 LLM 推理之外增加最小开销。VGE 前向传播每张图增加 0.3s，15 个因子计算增加 0.8s，belief 检索增加 0.05s（FAISS 近似最近邻）。每资产总决策延迟约 8.2s，完全在日频交易要求之内。Belief 存储查找相对 LLM 生成时间可忽略，确认非参数轨道不带来实际部署负担。
