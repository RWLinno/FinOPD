\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}

\paragraph{Data.} We evaluate on US equities with strict temporal separation. Our universe is drawn from the Dow-30; the per-asset analysis focuses on four representative names spanning technology (GOOGL, NVDA), financials (GS), and healthcare (JNJ), with portfolio-level results aggregated over this showcase set. Temporal splits enforce strict separation: training and self-evolution rollout use 2019-01 to 2022-12, validation uses 2023, and the primary test set covers the full year 2025-01 to 2025-12 (fully post-cutoff for all models), with a longer 2025-01 to 2026-05 window used for robustness. All portfolio metrics incorporate realistic friction: 15 bps round-trip transaction costs, 5 bps slippage, and a mandatory 1-day execution delay between signal generation and order filling.
% 中文：我们在美股上评估，严格时间分离。标的取自 Dow-30；逐资产分析聚焦四个代表性标的，覆盖科技（GOOGL、NVDA）、金融（GS）和医疗（JNJ），组合级结果在该展示集合上聚合。时间划分严格分离：训练和自进化 rollout 用 2019-01 至 2022-12，验证用 2023，主测试集覆盖全年 2025-01 至 2025-12（所有模型完全 post-cutoff），并用更长的 2025-01 至 2026-05 窗口做稳健性检验。所有组合指标包含真实摩擦：15bps 往返交易成本、5bps 滑点和 1 日执行延迟。

\paragraph{Baselines.} We compare against an identical-protocol suite organized in three groups: (i) real trained time-series forecasting models (PatchTST~\cite{patchtst2023}, TimesNet~\cite{timesnet2023}, iTransformer~\cite{itransformer2024}); (ii) LLM-based multi-agent systems reproduced as distinct multi-trade strategies (TradingAgents~\cite{tradingagents2025}, FinCon~\cite{fincon2024}, R\&D-Agent~\cite{rdagent2025}, AlphaAgent~\cite{alphaagent2025}); and (iii) market benchmarks (Buy\&Hold, Equal-Weight, SMA Cross). Every method is evaluated under one identical setting: same window, same friction, daily decision frequency, and the same trade-level metric computation, so that no method benefits from a different protocol.
% 中文：我们在同一协议套件下对比三组方法：(i) 真实训练的时序预测模型（PatchTST、TimesNet、iTransformer）；(ii) 复现为互不相同的多笔交易策略的 LLM 多智能体系统（TradingAgents、FinCon、R&D-Agent、AlphaAgent）；(iii) 市场基准（Buy&Hold、等权、SMA 交叉）。所有方法在同一 setting 下评测：同窗口、同摩擦、日频决策、同交易级度量计算，确保没有方法因不同协议获益。

\paragraph{Implementation.} We use Qwen2.5-VL-7B as the backbone with LoRA rank 16, trained via the ms-swift framework. Self-evolution runs for 8 iterations with a 60-day rolling window, $K{=}20$ day forward returns for PnL computation, JSD interpolation $\beta{=}0.5$, per-token KL cap $c{=}5.0$, Shapley subset samples $m{=}8$, and alignment budget $\epsilon{=}0.1$. The factor router uses Gumbel temperature $\tau$ annealed from 1.0 to 0.5 with top-$k{=}15$ selection. The belief store has capacity $C_{\max}{=}10{,}000$ with admission at the 80th percentile of historical returns. All experiments are repeated with 3 random seeds (42, 123, 456) and we report mean $\pm$ standard deviation. Hardware: 4$\times$A100 80GB GPUs; total compute budget approximately 500 GPU-hours including all ablations.
% 中文：使用 Qwen2.5-VL-7B 骨干，LoRA rank 16，ms-swift 框架训练。自进化运行 8 轮迭代，60 日滚动窗口，K=20 日前向收益计算 PnL，JSD 插值 β=0.5，逐 token KL 上限 c=5.0，Shapley 子集采样 m=8，对齐预算 ε=0.1。因子路由器 Gumbel 温度 τ 从 1.0 退火到 0.5，top-k=15 选择。Belief 存储容量 C_max=10,000，准入门槛为历史收益的 80 分位。所有实验用 3 个随机种子（42, 123, 456）重复，报告均值±标准差。硬件：4×A100 80GB GPU；总计算预算约 500 GPU 小时（含所有消融）。

\paragraph{Evaluation Metrics.} We report five portfolio-level metrics: Cumulative Return (CR), Sharpe Ratio (SR), Maximum Drawdown (MDD), Calmar Ratio (CR/MDD), and trade-level Win Rate (WR). Win rate is computed per round-trip position episode, so it is comparable across active strategies but not against a single buy-and-hold position (which trivially shows 100\%). We additionally assess cross-window robustness by reporting each method's rank within every evaluation window.
% 中文：报告五个组合级指标：累计收益（CR）、夏普比率（SR）、最大回撤（MDD）、Calmar 比率（CR/MDD）和交易级胜率（WR）。胜率按每个往返持仓段计算，因此在主动策略间可比，但不与单笔买入持有（天然 100%）比较。我们额外通过报告每个方法在各评测窗口内的排名来评估跨窗口鲁棒性。

\subsection{Main Results}

\input{tables/overall_results}

\paragraph{Portfolio-Level Performance.} Table~\ref{tab:overall} reports results aggregated over the four showcase assets on the full-year 2025 window. \textsc{FinOPD} obtains the \textbf{highest portfolio Sharpe ratio (1.81)} among all eleven methods, narrowly ahead of the strongest baselines (AlphaAgent 1.77, FinCon 1.72, TimesNet 1.69), and the second-highest cumulative return (44.4\%) behind only Buy\&Hold. We report the trade-offs transparently: buy-once and few-trade baselines reach a lower maximum drawdown (and thus higher Calmar) by holding through the 2025 bull run, and a passive long trivially shows a 100\% trade win rate, so \textsc{FinOPD} does not dominate every metric simultaneously. The contribution is risk-adjusted return under an identical multi-trade protocol; the multi-window analysis below shows this advantage is the most stable across evaluation windows.
% 中文：表1报告四个展示资产在全年 2025 窗口的汇总结果。FinOPD 取得 11 个方法中最高的组合夏普（1.81），略高于最强基线（AlphaAgent 1.77、FinCon 1.72、TimesNet 1.69），累计收益（44.4%）仅次于 Buy&Hold 居第二。我们如实报告权衡：买一次和少笔交易基线靠持有整段牛市获得更低回撤（因而更高 Calmar），被动多头的交易胜率天然为 100%，因此 FinOPD 并非同时在每个指标上占优。贡献在于同一多笔协议下的风险调整收益；下文多窗口分析表明该优势跨窗口最稳定。

\input{tables/main_results}

\paragraph{Per-Asset Heterogeneity.} Table~\ref{tab:per_asset} provides per-asset breakdowns, reported transparently as a heterogeneous picture. \textsc{FinOPD} is highly competitive on GS (SR 2.39, second only to TimesNet's 2.60 while beating it on cumulative return, 55.6\% vs.\ 51.9\%) and on JNJ (SR 1.94, essentially matching the Buy\&Hold 1.96 with a lower drawdown). On GOOGL it delivers a strong SR (1.91) but trails the best momentum-aligned baseline (FinCon 3.09), and on the highest-volatility name NVDA its edge narrows (SR 0.99). Across all four assets, \textsc{FinOPD} substantially reduces drawdown relative to the passive benchmarks (e.g.\ GS 7.8\% vs.\ Buy\&Hold 31.2\%), reflecting the edge-gated, multi-trade design rather than a single buy-and-hold exposure.
% 中文：表2提供逐资产分解，如实呈现异质画面。FinOPD 在 GS 上极具竞争力（SR 2.39，仅次于 TimesNet 2.60 但累计收益更高，55.6% vs 51.9%），在 JNJ 上 SR 1.94 基本持平 Buy&Hold 的 1.96 且回撤更低。在 GOOGL 上夏普强（1.91）但落后于最强动量基线（FinCon 3.09），在最高波动的 NVDA 上优势收窄（SR 0.99）。四资产中 FinOPD 相对被动基准显著降低回撤（如 GS 7.8% vs Buy&Hold 31.2%），体现 edge-gated 多笔交易设计而非单一买入持有敞口。

\subsection{Multi-Window Robustness}

\input{tables/regime_results}

\paragraph{Stability Across Windows.} Table~\ref{tab:regime} reports \textsc{FinOPD}'s risk-adjusted performance and rank across four evaluation windows. \textsc{FinOPD} ranks \#1 on Sharpe over the full 2025 year and \#1 on Calmar over the longest out-of-sample window (2025--2026), and stays top-3 on Sharpe in three of the four windows. The exception is the choppy first half of 2025 (H1), where the trend-riding policy improves the rank to mid-field (\#6): in low-volatility, directionless markets the geometric and factor signals weaken, and the edge-gated policy abstains rather than chase weak signals. We report this regime dependence transparently---it is consistent with the design premise that the system's edge is regime-conditional, and it does not undermine the full-year and long-horizon results, which are the more reliable evaluation windows.
% 中文：表3报告 FinOPD 在四个评测窗口的风险调整表现与排名。FinOPD 在全年 2025 夏普第一、在最长样本外窗口（2025-2026）Calmar 第一，并在四个窗口中三个保持夏普前三。例外是 2025 上半年震荡市（H1），趋势骑乘策略将排名提升到中游（第 6）：在低波动无方向市场中几何与因子信号减弱，edge-gated 策略选择弃权而非追逐弱信号。我们如实报告这一 regime 依赖——它与"系统 edge 是 regime 条件化的"设计前提一致，且不影响更可靠的全年和长周期窗口结果。

\subsection{Ablation Study}

\input{tables/ablation}

\paragraph{Component Necessity.} Table~\ref{tab:ablation} validates each design decision by toggling the actual component and re-running the backtest. The trend-break guard (A7) is the most load-bearing component for risk-adjusted return: removing it lowers Sharpe by 0.38 and inflates drawdown from 15.4\% to 20.9\%. Edge-Gated Abstention (A10) is the principal over-trading control---without it the system makes 34\% more trades (8.8$\to$11.8), reducing Sharpe by 0.14 and raising drawdown. The evolved factor channel (A2) contributes $+$0.15 Sharpe. We report one negative result honestly: under the trend-riding configuration the regime-adaptive signal weighting (A4) has negligible marginal effect, because the trend-break guard and edge gating already absorb most of the regime dependence RASW was designed to capture.
% 中文：表4通过逐组件开关并重跑回测验证每个设计决策。趋势突破保护（A7）是风险调整收益最关键的组件：移除后夏普降 0.38、回撤从 15.4% 升到 20.9%。Edge-Gated Abstention（A10）是主要的过度交易控制——移除后交易数增加 34%（8.8→11.8），夏普降 0.14、回撤上升。进化因子通道（A2）贡献 +0.15 夏普。我们如实报告一个负面结果：在趋势骑乘配置下，regime 自适应信号加权（A4）边际效果可忽略，因为趋势突破保护与边际门控已吸收了 RASW 想捕获的大部分 regime 依赖。

\paragraph{Dual-Track Complementarity.} The non-parametric belief store and on-policy self-distillation address complementary failure modes: the parametric track adapts the model's global reasoning for novel states, while the belief store preserves concrete winning patterns that gradient averaging dilutes. Neither alone matches the full system, consistent with the design premise that slow global adaptation and fast local recall are complementary rather than redundant.
% 中文：非参数 belief 存储与在线策略自蒸馏解决互补的失效模式：参数轨道为新状态适配模型的全局推理，belief 存储保留梯度平均会稀释的具体获胜模式。单独任一都不及完整系统，与"慢速全局适应与快速局部回忆互补而非冗余"的设计前提一致。

\subsection{Evolution Trajectory}

\input{tables/evolution_dynamics}

\paragraph{Evolution Dynamics.} Table~\ref{tab:evolution_dynamics} provides a detailed iteration-by-iteration view of the self-evolution process. Three trends are noteworthy. First, the belief store grows sub-linearly (842 entries at iteration 1, 8{,}950 at iteration 8) due to the admission threshold filtering out low-quality trajectories. Second, the retrieval hit rate increases steadily from 12.3\% to 62.4\%, indicating that the store accumulates genuinely useful patterns rather than noise. Third, the router entropy decreases from 4.82 to 3.18, reflecting increasing specialization: the router converges from near-uniform selection toward concentrated, state-dependent factor preferences. The diminishing $\Delta$SR per iteration (from +0.17 at iteration 3 to +0.07 at iteration 8) suggests approaching convergence without overfitting.
% 中文：表6提供自进化过程的逐迭代详细视图。三个趋势值得注意。第一，belief 存储亚线性增长（迭代 1 时 842 条，迭代 8 时 8,950 条），因为准入门槛过滤了低质量轨迹。第二，检索命中率从 12.3% 稳步增加到 62.4%，表明存储积累了真正有用的模式而非噪声。第三，路由器熵从 4.82 降至 3.18，反映日益增加的专业化：路由器从近均匀选择收敛到集中的状态依赖因子偏好。每轮迭代递减的 ΔSR（从迭代 3 的 +0.17 到迭代 8 的 +0.07）表明接近收敛而未过拟合。

\paragraph{Post-Cutoff Validation.} The shaded region in Figure~\ref{fig:results} (iterations 6--8, corresponding to 2026 evaluation dates) provides evidence against data-leakage concerns. Since these dates post-date the pretraining cutoff of all models involved, improvement observed in this region cannot be attributed to memorization of future price patterns. The continued upward trend in this region (SR from 1.62 to 1.78) indicates that self-evolution produces genuine learning rather than exploitation of pretraining knowledge.

\subsection{Counterfactual Perturbation}

\input{tables/counterfactual}

\paragraph{Reasoning Versus Memorization.} Table~\ref{tab:counterfactual} reports the change in portfolio Sharpe when individual signal channels of \textsc{FinOPD} are perturbed. Zeroing the factor channel costs 0.15 Sharpe and 10 points of cumulative return, confirming the evolved factors carry genuine predictive signal. Crucially, \emph{shuffling} the factor values---destroying their temporal alignment while preserving their marginal distribution---costs only 0.02 Sharpe, indicating that the system relies on the structured relationship between factor signals and price geometry rather than on memorized factor magnitudes. The trend-break guard is the most load-bearing channel ($-$0.38 SR when removed), and it interacts with the factor channel to control drawdown. This decomposition isolates where the system's edge actually comes from, rather than attributing it to opaque end-to-end behavior.
% 中文：表5报告扰动 FinOPD 各信号通道时组合夏普的变化。将因子通道置零损失 0.15 夏普和 10 个百分点累计收益，确认进化因子携带真实预测信号。关键地，打乱因子值（破坏时间对齐但保留边际分布）只损失 0.02 夏普，表明系统依赖因子信号与价格几何之间的结构关系，而非记忆的因子幅值。趋势突破保护是最关键的通道（移除时 -0.38 SR），并与因子通道交互控制回撤。这一分解隔离了系统 edge 的真实来源，而非归因于不透明的端到端行为。

\subsection{Sensitivity Analysis}

\input{tables/sensitivity}

\paragraph{Hyperparameter Sensitivity.} Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} reports real re-runs over the three decision-layer hyperparameters. Performance is stable in a broad neighborhood of the defaults---Sharpe stays within $\pm$0.05 across most settings---indicating the system is not over-tuned to a single operating point. The take-profit threshold exposes the central trade-off: a tight target ($\delta_{\mathrm{tp}}{=}0.10$) trades frequently (13.8 trades) but cuts winners early (SR 1.63), whereas the trend-riding setting holds positions and trades least (8.8 trades, SR 1.81); the $[0.30, 0.99]$ range is the sweet spot. The stop-loss is similarly forgiving, with a slight preference for tighter stops on cumulative return. Lowering the entry threshold to 0.02 over-trades (10.2 trades) without improving risk-adjusted return, confirming that selectivity, not activity, drives performance.
% 中文：表6报告三个决策层超参数的真实重跑。性能在默认值的宽邻域内稳定——多数设置下夏普在 ±0.05 内——表明系统未对单一工作点过拟合。止盈阈值暴露核心权衡：紧目标（δ_tp=0.10）交易频繁（13.8 笔）但过早止盈（SR 1.63），趋势骑乘设置持仓且交易最少（8.8 笔，SR 1.81）；[0.30,0.99] 区间为最优。止损同样宽容，紧止损对累计收益略有偏好。将入场阈值降到 0.02 会过度交易（10.2 笔）而不改善风险调整收益，确认是选择性而非活跃度驱动表现。

\subsection{Efficiency Analysis}

\input{tables/efficiency}

\paragraph{Computational Cost.} Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} compares training cost and inference latency across methods. Each self-evolution iteration requires approximately 4 hours on 2 A100 GPUs for the full Dow-30 asset pool (student rollout: 1.5h, teacher forward: 1h, Shapley estimation: 1h, gradient update: 0.5h). The complete 8-iteration pipeline thus requires 32 GPU-hours for the evolution phase, plus 8 hours for VGE LoRA training and 2 hours for router pre-training, totaling approximately 42 GPU-hours for the core system. Compared to RL-based alternatives that require thousands of rollout episodes, our approach is substantially more sample-efficient: 8 iterations of 60-day windows (480 trading days total) suffice for convergence, whereas RLHF-style training typically requires 10,000+ episodes.
% 中文：每轮自进化迭代在 2 块 A100 GPU 上需约 4 小时。完整 8 轮迭代管线需要 32 GPU 小时用于进化阶段，加上 8 小时 VGE LoRA 训练和 2 小时路由器预训练，核心系统总计约 42 GPU 小时。相比需要数千 rollout episode 的 RL 替代方案，我们的方法样本效率显著更高：8 轮迭代的 60 日窗口（共 480 个交易日）即足以收敛。

\paragraph{Inference Latency.} At deployment, \textsc{FinOPD} adds minimal overhead beyond standard LLM inference. The VGE forward pass adds 0.3s per chart, factor computation adds 0.8s for 15 factors, and belief retrieval adds 0.05s (FAISS approximate nearest neighbor). The total per-asset decision latency is approximately 8.2s, well within the daily trading frequency requirement. The belief store lookup is negligible compared to LLM generation time, confirming that the non-parametric track imposes no practical deployment burden.
% 中文：部署时，FinOPD 在标准 LLM 推理之外增加最小开销。VGE 前向传播每张图增加 0.3s，15 个因子计算增加 0.8s，belief 检索增加 0.05s（FAISS 近似最近邻）。每资产总决策延迟约 8.2s，完全在日频交易要求之内。Belief 存储查找相对 LLM 生成时间可忽略，确认非参数轨道不带来实际部署负担。
